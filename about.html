Our story

Every piece
has a past.

We started The Thrift Hub to make great clothes last longer — and to make thrifting feel as good as buying new.

Scroll ↓

Fast fashion makes clothes to be thrown away. We do the opposite. We dig through bales, markets and closets to find pieces worth a second life then make sure they arrive clean, honest and ready to wear.

How a piece gets to you

01 — Sourced

Hand-picked

We go through hundreds of pieces to find the few that are worth it — strong fabric, good cut, real character.

02 — Cleaned

Washed & steamed

Every piece is washed, de-pilled, steamed and checked seam by seam before it ever gets photographed.

03 — Graded

Honestly graded

A = like new. B = light wear. C = visible character. Any flaw is photographed and written down. No surprises.

04 — Shipped

To your door

Packed in recycled mailers and dispatched within 48 hours. Cash on delivery available nationwide.
