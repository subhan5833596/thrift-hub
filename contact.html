FAQ

Is every piece really one of one?
Yes. Thrift means each item is unique. Once it sells, it's gone — we never restock the exact same piece.
What do the grades A / B / C mean?
A — like new, no visible wear. B — light wear like small fading. C — visible character (a mark, a repair). Any flaw is shown in the photos and written in the description.
How do I know my size?
Tag sizes vary across brands and decades, so we measure every piece flat. Compare the measurements with a piece you already own.
Do you offer cash on delivery?
Yes, COD is available nationwide. You can also pay by bank transfer or card.
Can I return a piece?
If something doesn't match its description, message us within 3 days of delivery and we'll sort it out.
How long does shipping take?
We dispatch within 48 hours. Delivery usually takes 2–5 working days depending on your city.
